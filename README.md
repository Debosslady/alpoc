# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Asset-Link Petroleum Optimization Company (ALPOC) — One-Page Corporate Landing Page

### 1. Product Overview

Design and develop a **modern, premium, responsive one-page corporate landing page** for **Asset-Link Petroleum Optimization Company (ALPOC), RC 1393662**, using the supplied company logo as the primary brand asset.

The website should communicate ALPOC as a credible, technology-driven Nigerian oil & gas company providing integrated petroleum optimization, asset development, crude marketing, engineering, financing, logistics, and manpower solutions across Nigeria and sub-Saharan Africa.

### 2. Design Direction

* **Style:** Modern, professional, corporate, premium, clean and technology-driven.
* **Typography:** Modern **Sans-Serif**, visually matching geometric/grotesque fonts such as **Arial, Helvetica, or Open Sans**.
* **Primary Colors:** Red, Deep Blue, Light Blue.
* **Secondary Color:** Green.
* **Neutral:** Black, white/light neutral backgrounds.
* Use the **uploaded ALPOC logo** prominently in the navigation/header and footer.
* Maintain generous whitespace, strong visual hierarchy, subtle shadows, clean cards, rounded elements and professional oil & gas imagery.
* Avoid excessive gradients, clutter, cartoonish graphics or overly decorative elements.
* Ensure excellent readability and accessibility on desktop, tablet and mobile.

#### E. Core Portfolio of Services

Present services in a modern responsive grid with icons and short descriptions.

1. **Crude Lifting & Marketing** — Crude sales partnerships, vessel fleets, storage, floating production and modular refinery swap deals.
2. **Asset Development & Reservoir Management** — Basin analysis, evaluation, reserves, asset modelling, FDP and production performance analysis.
3. **Production Optimization** — System debottlenecking, integrated well/reservoir/facility modelling and sensitivity analysis.
4. **Well Intervention Solutions** — Water/gas shut-off, zone switches, reperforation, sand control, gas lift, slickline, coiled tubing and related interventions.
5. **Production Operation Surveillance** — Remote near-real-time monitoring connected to intelligent asset solutions.
6. **Wellhead Equipment & Maintenance** — Wellhead assemblies, Christmas trees, casing heads, stuffing boxes, flow meters and related equipment.
7. **Drilling, Completion & Workover** — Land, swamp and offshore rigs, directional drilling, geosteering, MWD/LWD, cementing and casing inspection.
8. **Project Financing & Petroleum Economics** — Financing viable oilfield development projects and providing economic advisory services.
9. **Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC)** — Process facilities, pipelines, commissioning, repairs, valve replacement, corrosion protection and pigging.
10. **Logistics & Equipment Leasing** — Heavy-duty land equipment and marine assets including barges, tugboats, houseboats and vessels.
11. **Manpower & Project Management** — Technical and managerial manpower, visa/work permit support, contract evaluation and project execution.

Use **View More / Learn More** interaction if needed, while keeping the page visually uncluttered.
